# Prompt mẫu pixel V4

Công cụ: imagegen tích hợp, chỉnh từ concept mới V3. Đầu ra: public/design-preview/mmorpg-pixel-v4/styleboard-refined-v4.png. Mục đích: mẫu định hướng, không phải sprite sheet.

## Prompt đã dùng

Refine this ORIGINAL AN THANH pixel RPG style board as version 4. Increase detail only a SMALL amount, about 15-20 percent richer than the supplied version 3. Preserve the same village scene, top-down slightly oblique square-grid RPG perspective, character proportions, overall layout, muted medieval palette, and uncluttered playable ground. Do NOT return to ornate, dense fantasy illustration. Keep genuine hard stepped pixel clusters, no smooth gradients or painterly detail. Main scene: trees gain a few additional medium-sized leaf clusters and a couple of visible branches while retaining large readable canopy masses; cottages gain a simple roof ridge, occasional broken/shaded shingle variation, wooden door panels and a modest window sill; bridge gains a few age marks; stream gains restrained grouped ripples and bank stones, not foam everywhere. Add a FEW placed tufts and flowers at edges only, no wallpaper texture over the path or grass. Right reference panel: swordsman gains readable armor joints, a small cloak fold and sword guard; archer gains belt pouches and two visible arrow fletchings; mage gains one robe trim and subtle cloth folds. Keep about 3-head-tall stylized body, roughly 40 logical pixels high in intended production, not realistic anatomy or chibi baby. Wolf gains a few angular fur clumps; tree, chest and rock gain modest material accents. Use 4 tonal steps per main material, with an occasional fifth accent only on focal subjects; pixel cleanup and disciplined palette. No elaborate filigree, jewelry, big glow, bloom, atmospheric haze, waterfall, cliff spectacle or huge extra props. The target is a moderately rich production-friendly indie pixel RPG, a little more developed than V3, substantially simpler and clearer than a highly detailed fantasy illustration. Keep all samples fully in frame, palette swatches at bottom, no labels, no watermark. This is a single concept/style board, not a functional spritesheet.

## Giới hạn

Ảnh concept chưa xác nhận kích thước native, palette, frame, tile seams hoặc animation. V4 là tinh chỉnh mẫu theo phản hồi; chưa tuyên bố người dùng đã chấp thuận hoặc đã có bộ asset game hoàn chỉnh.
