# Prompt mẫu pixel tối giản V2

Công cụ: imagegen tích hợp, chế độ chỉnh ảnh concept mớiV1. Không dùng ảnh gói game cũ. Đầu ra public/design-preview/mmorpg-pixel-v2/styleboard-simple-v2.png. Đây là mẫu phong cách, không phải sprite sheet hoàn chỉnh.

## Prompt đã dùng

Redesign this ORIGINAL RPG style board to be DRAMATICALLY SIMPLER, genuinely sparse low-resolution pixel game art. Keep only the idea of a starter village and a few matching character/prop samples, redraw all shapes. Target approachable simple indie pixel RPG, NOT ornate fantasy illustration. Pixel clusters must be very LARGE and obvious, like a 320x180 native resolution screenshot enlarged 4x with nearest neighbor. Top-down orthographic with slight front faces, NOT isometric. On left two-thirds show a small patch of flat green grass with EMPTY QUIET GROUND, a tan path, TWO tiny simple cottages (plain flat brown roofs without individual shingles or wood grain), THREE trees made of ONE rounded angular canopy mass each with just TWO green shade regions, a narrow FLAT teal stream with only 2-3 simple white ripple pixels, and a tiny flat stone bridge. NO waterfall, no cliffs, no moss textures, no dense flowers, no individual grass blades, no atmospheric light, no glow, no gradient, no realistic stone patterns, no tiny objects crowding the village. One small player on path, simple shield and sword, clear silhouette, approximately 24-32 native pixels tall, 2.5-head proportions, only 3 colors per material and simple dot eyes. Right third on PLAIN dark green background show generous spaced samples: simple gray armored swordsman with muted teal tunic; simple brown leather archer; simple blue robe mage with plain staff and NO glow; a small blocky gray wolf; ONE small green slime; below them ONE simple tree, ONE plain gray rock, ONE tiny wooden chest. No huge boss, no huge armor details, no jewellery. Restrained palette of about 16-20 visible colors total: flat moss greens, muted brown earth, slate gray, cream highlights, teal water. Hard pixel edges, low detail, no anti aliasing. Bottom has 8 simple square color swatches. This must look like an actual easy-to-produce small pixel game asset kit, not concept illustration with thousands of details. No text, no watermark.

## Giới hạn

Ảnh tạo ra có phong cách đơn giản hơnV1 nhưng không tự chứng minh native pixel size, số màu chính xác, độ kín alpha/tile nối hoặc animation. Cần sản xuất/cleanup riêng theo ART-01V2.1 và ART-02V1.1. Không tính ảnhconcept thành bộ asset đã hoàn tất.
