# Prompt mẫu pixel chi tiết vừa V3

Công cụ: imagegen tích hợp. Chỉnh từ concept mới V2, không dùng asset game cũ. Đầu ra: public/design-preview/mmorpg-pixel-v3/styleboard-balanced-v3.png. Chỉ là mẫu định hướng, chưa phải sprite/tilemap hoàn chỉnh.

## Prompt đã dùng

Create version 3 of this ORIGINAL AN THANH RPG art-direction board, a balanced MEDIUM-DETAIL pixel RPG style between ornate illustration and extremely flat beginner pixel art. Edit the supplied version 2 board, keep its village beside a stream and its right-hand character/prop comparison layout, but make it richer and more mature while still clear and easy to produce as pixel game art. This is a concept style guide, not a working tileset or sprite animation. Top-down slightly oblique square-grid 2D RPG perspective, not isometric. Left two-thirds: two modest timber and plaster cottages, roofs with just a FEW broad rows of shingle shapes, visible timber beams and simple window frames, a gently curving stream with a few grouped pixel ripples and some simple shore stones, low stone bridge with a few readable stone blocks, winding tan path with a few stones, three rounded angular trees with three or four broad leaf clusters each, one or two small flower/grass clumps near buildings, quiet uncluttered walkable ground. No waterfall, dense forest or huge cliffs. Right third: one silver armored swordsman in a muted teal tabard with simple shoulder plates, belt and boots; a brown-and-green archer with visible quiver and bow; a muted blue mage with a cream collar and staff. Characters roughly 40 logical pixels tall, about 3 heads tall, clear readable silhouettes, no tiny filigree, no elaborate armor. Below them a small wolf with two fur shade regions, a slime, a tree, one rock and a wooden chest with simple metal bands. Use authentic stepped pixel edges, deliberate pixel clusters and 3-4 shades per material, a restrained palette about 24-32 colors, no smooth gradients, anti aliasing, blurred textures, photorealism or painterly brushwork. Subtle upper-left light and compact ground shadows, NO bloom or glow. Make the result charming, grounded and moderately detailed: not toy-like, not flat geometric clip art, and not high-detail fantasy concept illustration. Main scene scale must feel like a coherent playable pixel RPG. Simple palette swatches along bottom. No text or watermark.

## Giới hạn

Mẫu thể hiện mức chi tiết để so sánh; không xác nhận native pixel size, giới hạn palette, alpha, tile seams hoặc animation. V1 quá cầu kỳ và V2 quá đơn giản là lịch sử. V3 là hướng làm việc đang điều chỉnh, không ghi là người dùng đã duyệt.
