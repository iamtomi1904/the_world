Prompt đã dùng:

Create a completely ORIGINAL cohesive art-direction reference board for AN THANH, a medieval fantasy 2D top-down pixel-art open-world RPG. Do NOT use or imitate any previously supplied character art or game assets. This is one illustrated style guide board, NOT a functional spritesheet. Landscape composition on dark forest-green background. STRICT true low-resolution pixel-art aesthetic enlarged with nearest-neighbor look: visible square pixel clusters, hard stepped edges, compact 3-tone shading, no anti-aliasing, no smooth gradients, no painterly brushwork, no 3D render, no glossy mobile-game illustration. Use approximately 480x270 logical pixel density enlarged across board. Top-down slightly oblique RPG perspective, square terrain grid, 32-pixel tile visual module. Main left two-thirds: a compact believable new starter village beside a shallow winding brook, stone bridge, small timber-and-stone cottages, trees made of clear blocky pixel clusters, path and low cliff, hidden wooden chest partly behind rocks, a few tiny villagers, a newly designed modest silver-armored swordsman in muted teal tabard with a plain sword, a leather-clad archer and a robe-wearing mage with subtle amber magic. Human bodies around 48 logical pixels high when shown in separate comparison panel; village figures naturally smaller relative to main scene. Right third arranged as simple separated sample panels: standing full-body swordsman, small gray wolf and larger original moss-stone guardian, oak tree, rock, wooden chest; bottom strip 12 discrete color swatches. Grounded medieval palette moss green, ochre earth, slate stone, desaturated teal water, restrained warm lantern gold; clean readable silhouettes and consistent pixel density. Light from upper left, short ground shadows. No huge bloom, no ornate realism, no lettering, no watermark, no fake grid lines drawn over sprites. All subjects separated with generous margins and fully within the image. Convey an authentic handcrafted pixel RPG world, not the old existing assets.

Giới hạn quan sát: ảnh là concept có nhiều chi tiết và góc nhìn minh họa; không phải bằng chứng kích thước/frame/grid tuân chuẩn sản xuất. Cần tạo sprite/tile riêng và cleanup ở pixel density mục tiêu.
