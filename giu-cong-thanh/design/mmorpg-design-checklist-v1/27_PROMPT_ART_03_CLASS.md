## Prompt đã dùng

Using the attached ORIGINAL AN THANH styleboard V4 ONLY as visual style reference, create ONE new class identity concept board in the SAME moderately detailed, readable pixel art style. No old game sprites. This is art direction, not a working spritesheet. Three equal columns, ample separation, plain dark green background. TOP ROW three new full-body adult-proportioned stylized 3-head-high RPG adventurers facing front slightly angled: LEFT WARRIOR with broad compact shoulder silhouette, simple worn steel armor, short muted teal tabard, BOTH HANDS holding a broad heavy greatsword at low diagonal beside body, no shield, no horn helmet, no ornate knight filigree; CENTER RANGER with narrow agile silhouette, cropped leather mantle, olive green and warm brown practical clothing, clearly visible curved bow held to side, arrow quiver projecting above opposite shoulder, boots, no giant cape; RIGHT MAGE with simple slate-blue long robe forming a bell-shaped hem, warm rust-red inner collar/sash for a beginner fire-school motif, unhooded or small folded hood leaving face visible, a tall plain wood staff with a modest amber prism head, small book at belt, NO luminous fire or particle effects. All three SAME anatomical scale, body heights approx 40 logical production pixels, deliberate stepped pixels, 4 shades per material, steel/wood/cloth differentiated using compact clusters, restrained V4 detail, no realistic rendering. MIDDLE ROW directly under each colored character show the exact same pose as a flat solid ivory silhouette, preserving gaps between weapon and body so class reads with color removed. BOTTOM ROW directly under each silhouette show a full-color rear view of the SAME character, consistent identity and equipment, particularly both sword-hand placement, ranger quiver and mage robe/staff. Keep every body and weapon fully within its column with clear dark margins. Do not include icons, village, weapons not described, mounts or extra actors. NO text, numbers, labels, watermark, glow, smooth gradients, blur or painterly texture. Polished production-friendly pixel concept sheet matching original V4, not overly flat and not more ornate.
